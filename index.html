Climate transparency app

Do climate promises match the carbon?

RE100 is a mobile app for checking whether companies' renewable-energy claims line up with what they actually report — with ESG evaluations, sector comparisons and real case studies in your pocket.

Coming soon to iPhone and Android

What's in the app

Built for people who want to look past the headlines and see the evidence behind a company's sustainability story.

Company profiles

RE100 progress, ESG score and grade, certificate (REC) reliance and target year for companies across eight sectors, each compared with its sector average.

Authenticity checks

In-depth evaluations of major tech and semiconductor companies across 15 environmental, social and governance criteria, with the evidence and source behind each result.

Case study library

Short case studies on how companies from BASF to Patagonia are tackling climate and materials challenges.

Greenwashing signals

A simple status — leader, moderate progress, transition challenges or high risk — shows where claims and emissions diverge.

Who it's for

RE100 started as a student-led project to make corporate climate data easier to understand.

  • Students and teachersExplore real ESG data for class projects, debates and research.
  • Curious consumersCheck the brands you buy from before trusting a green label.
  • Young professionalsGet a quick, evidence-based read on a company's climate track record.

Educational use only. Company information is compiled from public disclosures and independent research. It is not investment, financial or legal advice. Spotted an error? Let us know.
