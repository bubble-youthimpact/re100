Frequently asked questions

Is the app free?

Yes. RE100 is free, has no ads and no in-app purchases.

Do I need an account?

No. You can use every feature without signing up.

Where does the company data come from?

From companies' own public disclosures — sustainability reports, proxy statements and RE100 progress data — plus independent research. Each evaluation in the app shows its source and reporting period.

How are the ESG scores calculated?

Each company is reviewed against 15 criteria across environment, social and governance. Some scores are calculated from defined scoring bands; others are estimates based on the review outcome and are labelled "est." in the app. Criteria without enough evidence stay pending and are never counted as zero.

Is this investment advice?

No. RE100 is an educational tool. Please don't use it as the basis for financial decisions.

I found an error in a company's data.

Thank you — please email us the company name, what looks wrong and, if you have it, a link to the correct source. We review every report.
